What’s in the audit

  • Every Connect module mapped to its Forge equivalent and rated: direct, rework, high risk or no equivalent.
  • Blockers first: custom fields with existing data, static macros, themes, blueprints, search request views.
  • Scopes and consent: which granular Forge scopes you need and what admins will be asked on upgrade.
  • Webhooks to Forge triggers, event by event.
  • A risk-ordered plan with an effort range in developer days.
  • Step-1 manifest.yml: adopt Forge with the same app key and keep your Connect modules, so customers keep their installs and data and you can ship updates again.
  • The road to zero Connect modules. Atlassian treats an app as Connect until the last Connect module is gone, so the plan covers every one of them.

Questions

Do you need access to our code?

Not for the audit. Everything comes from the descriptor your app already publishes. For step 1 and full migrations we work in your repository with access you control.

How do you do it so fast?

Our own analyser reads the descriptor and maps every module. A person then reviews each audit before it goes out.

Is our information kept private?

Yes. Your audit is sent only to you, and we never publish anything about a single app.

Will our customers have to reinstall?

No. Step 1 keeps the same app key, so installs, licences and data stay where they are. Admins may be asked to approve new permissions on upgrade, and the audit tells you which.

We’d rather stop maintaining the app.

Then tell us. We also take over apps from vendors who no longer want to run them.

Who are you?

Flowsmiths, an independent Marketplace vendor that builds Forge apps for Jira, including Expiry Tracker. You deal with Tomas Bindac directly.
